# The Break-Up (2006)
**The Break-Up** is an American romantic comedy-drama film directed by **Peyton Reed** and released in **2006**. It stars **Vince Vaughn** as Gary Grobowski and **Jennifer Aniston** as Brooke Meyers.

## Plot Summary
The story follows Gary and Brooke, a couple who decide to end their relationship. However, because they jointly own a condominium, neither wants to move out. As they continue living together, their breakup turns into a series of arguments, mind games, and attempts to make each other jealous. 

## Interesting Fact
Unlike many romantic comedies, *The Break-Up* presents a more realistic look at relationship problems and the challenges couples face when communication breaks down. The film was a commercial success, earning over **$205 million worldwide** against a **$52 million budget**.
